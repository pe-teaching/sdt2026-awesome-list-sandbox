# Fun and hobbies

## Games and puzzles

- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com) - a programming puzzle every day in December, fun to solve together with classmates.
- [Lichess](https://lichess.org) - free and open-source chess site with puzzles and no ads.
- [GeoGuessr](https://www.geoguessr.com) - guess the place on a street photo, a fun way to learn geography.

## Music

- [Ultimate Guitar](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com) - chords and tabs for most songs, a good start to play the guitar.

## Sport

- [Strava](https://www.strava.com) - track runs and bike rides and join clubs, also with classmates.
- [MuseScore](https://musescore.org) — write sheet music.
- [Chess puzzles](http://lichess.org/no-such-page-404) - short puzzles to train chess tactics every day in a break.
