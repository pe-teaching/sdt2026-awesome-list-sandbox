# Life and everyday

- [Splitwise](https://www.splitwise.com) - track shared costs with flatmates, so nobody has to remember who paid.
- [Budget Bytes](https://www.budgetbytes.com) - cheap and simple recipes for students who just started to cook for themselves.
- [Pomofocus](https://pomofocus.io) - a simple pomodoro timer in the browser that helps to focus on homework.
- [Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org) - clear advice on sleep, useful when exams and deadlines come at once.
